DUKANS INDIA - Delete Account Instructions

⚠️ Account Deletion Notice

Deleting your DUKANS account is a permanent action. Once confirmed, your personal profile, addresses, cart items, and preferences will be permanently erased immediately.

📱 Step-by-Step Instructions: How to Delete Your Account From the DUKANS App

1
Open the DUKANS App: Launch the DUKANS Android Application on your device and log in to your account.
2
Go to Profile Page: Tap on the "Profile" tab located at the bottom right corner of the main screen navigation bar.
3
Access Legal Pages: Scroll down the Profile menu to the "Legal Pages" section and select "Delete Account".
4
Confirm Terms: Review the data deletion details and check the agreement box: "I understand that deleting my DUKANS account is permanent and cannot be undone."
5
Tap "Delete My Account": Tap the red "Delete My Account" button and confirm the final confirmation prompt. Your account and associated cloud data will be deleted instantly.

📊 What Data Will Be Erased Immediately

  • Personal Profile: Full name, mobile number, email address, gender, and profile photo.
  • Saved Addresses: All saved delivery addresses, flat numbers, and landmarks.
  • Cart & Search History: Active shopping cart items, saved preferences, and search queries.
  • Notifications & Support: In-app notification inbox and support callback logs.

📋 Data Retained For Legal & Tax Compliance

In accordance with applicable Indian statutory requirements, tax regulations, and auditing standards, completed order invoices and financial accounting records are retained for statutory retention periods.
