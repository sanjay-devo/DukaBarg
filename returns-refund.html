Returns, Replacements & Refund Policy

Effective Date: May 2025 | Version 1.0

1. Overview & Customer Assurance

At DUKANS INDIA, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. We maintain strict quality checks across product storage, packaging, and delivery. This policy outlines the conditions under which items may be returned, replaced, or refunded.

2. Return & Replacement Eligibility

Products are eligible for return or free replacement if:

  • The delivered product is physically damaged, leaked, or corrupted upon arrival.
  • The product delivered is incorrect or differs significantly from what was ordered.
  • The product has passed its printed expiration/best-before date upon delivery.
  • Essential items or packages are missing from your delivered order.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following product categories are non-returnable due to hygiene, safety, and health regulations:

  • Perishable groceries, fresh dairy, fruits, vegetables, and frozen items once delivered and accepted in good condition.
  • Unsealed personal care, wellness, or baby hygiene products.
  • Products damaged due to post-delivery mishandling or improper domestic storage by the customer.

4. Reporting an Issue & Timelines

  • Delivery Verification: Customers are encouraged to inspect goods immediately upon delivery.
  • Reporting Window: Issues with damaged, expired, or wrong items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
  • How to Report: Request an instant support callback via the "Help & Support" screen in the app or reach out to our team at +91-9203251821 / india@dukans.in.

5. Evidence & Verification Requirements

To process return or refund requests efficiently, DUKANS INDIA customer support may request clear photographic proof or a short video demonstrating the defect/damage alongside the order tax invoice.

6. Return Pickup & Replacement Process

Upon verification by our support executive, a free pickup of the damaged product or free dispatch of a fresh replacement product will be initiated within 24 to 48 hours.

7. Refund Approval & Processing Timelines

  • Refund Destination: Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method used during checkout (UPI, bank account, or payment card via Razorpay).
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): For COD orders, refunds are credited via UPI or direct bank transfer as agreed with the customer.
  • Refund Timeline: Once approved, refunds are credited within 5 to 7 business days depending on your bank settlement processing schedule.

8. Failed, Duplicate & Dropped Payments

If payment is debited from your bank account for a dropped or unconfirmed order, Razorpay automatically initiates an automated reconciliation. The debited amount is credited back to your original payment source within 3 to 5 business days.

9. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to fulfillment through the "My Orders" page or by requesting a Support Callback.
  • Post-Dispatch Cancellation: Once the delivery executive is out for delivery, cancellations may be subject to a nominal logistics fee recovery.
