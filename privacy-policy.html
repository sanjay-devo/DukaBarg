DUKANS INDIA

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: May 2025 | Version 1.0

1. Overview & Commitment

DUKANS INDIA ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, and protect your data when you use the DUKANS Android mobile application and associated online digital marketplace services in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable digital governance standards of India.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or interact with DUKANS INDIA, we collect only the necessary data required for marketplace operations:

  • Account Profile: Full name, 10-digit mobile number, email address, and gender provided during registration.
  • Delivery Details: Saved delivery addresses including house/flat details, street, city, state, pincode, and contact numbers.
  • Orders & Transactions: Items purchased, order quantities, delivery timestamps, total amounts, and payment status.
  • Payment References: Payment gateway transaction IDs, Razorpay order references, and payment method selected (UPI, netbanking, card, or COD). We DO NOT collect or store full credit/debit card details, CVVs, or bank passwords.
  • Support Communications: Customer support callback requests, language preferences, and resolution logs.
  • Device & Technical Identifiers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens, platform identifier (Android), and app version for push notifications and account security.

3. Purposes of Data Collection

Your information is utilized strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Account Creation & Authentication: Verifying user identities via Firebase Phone OTP or Google Sign-In.
  • Order Fulfillment & Delivery: Processing orders, dispatching shipments, and enabling delivery partner navigation.
  • Payment Gateway Processing: Processing transactions securely via Razorpay.
  • Customer Support: Connecting callback requests with our support executives in your chosen language.
  • Notifications: Sending essential order status updates, delivery alerts, and promotional announcements.
  • Security & Legal Compliance: Preventing fraudulent activity, resolving disputes, and complying with statutory laws.

4. Firebase & Third-Party Service Providers

DUKANS INDIA integrates verified, high-security third-party infrastructure services to power the application:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure login sessions, Phone OTP verification, and Google OAuth credentials.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Serves as our primary cloud datastore for user profiles, saved addresses, active carts, and notification history.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers real-time push notifications to your Android device.
  • Firebase Storage: Safely hosts profile photos uploaded by users.
  • Razorpay Payment Gateway: Securely handles all online payment processing in compliance with PCI-DSS guidelines.

5. Payment Information Security

All digital payment transactions are conducted directly through Razorpay’s encrypted and PCI-DSS certified gateway. DUKANS INDIA never sees, records, or stores your sensitive banking credentials, card numbers, or UPI PINs on our servers.

6. Data Sharing & Third-Party Access

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Data is shared exclusively with necessary service partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Logistics & Delivery Partners: Delivery address and contact number for order fulfillment.
  • Payment Processor (Razorpay): Transaction amounts and reference IDs for payment authorization.
  • Statutory & Legal Authorities: When mandatory under applicable Indian legislation or lawful court orders.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your personal profile data, addresses, search history, and cart items are retained while your account remains active. You may permanently delete your DUKANS account at any time through the "Delete Account" page in the Legal Pages section.

Please note that completed order records, financial tax invoices, and accounting audit logs are retained for mandatory statutory periods required under Indian tax laws, even after account deletion.

8. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption standards (TLS/HTTPS) for all data in transit. Access to Firebase Realtime Database and storage nodes is strictly controlled via authenticated Firebase Security Rules.
