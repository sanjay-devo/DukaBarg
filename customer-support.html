📱 In-App Instant Callback Feature

You can request an instant support call back directly through the DUKANS Android Application:

  1. Open the DUKANS App and go to the "Profile" tab.
  2. Tap on "Help & Support / Callback".
  3. Select your query topic (Order Status, Missing Item, Refund Request) and your preferred language.
  4. Tap "Request Instant Callback" to connect with an executive.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How can I track my live order?
Go to the "My Orders" tab inside the DUKANS app to view real-time delivery status, partner details, and expected arrival time.
2. What should I do if an item is missing or damaged?
Report any damaged or missing product within 24 hours via the Support section in the app or call us at +91-9203251821 for immediate replacement or refund.
3. How long does a refund take to process?
Approved refunds are credited directly back to your original payment source (UPI, Card, or Netbanking) within 5 to 7 working days.
